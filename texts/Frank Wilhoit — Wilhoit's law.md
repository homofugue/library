Conservatism consists of exactly one proposition, to wit: There must be in-groups whom the law protects but does not bind, alongside out-groups whom the law binds but does not protect.

Usually quoted as the second sentence alone and attributed to the wrong Wilhoit; the author is the composer Frank Wilhoit, writing in a blog comment thread. The pasted version (“Wilhoit's Law strikes again. …”) is someone's later citation of it.
