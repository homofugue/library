Trauma splits the psyche into a helper and a helped, Ferenczi writes in his last year. This walk follows the split through four analysts and two writers.

1. [[Ferenczi — traumatic aloneness]] — 1932: the being left alone must help himself, and so splits into one who helps and one who is helped.
2. [[Winnicott — the squiggle game and string]] — A boy turns every squiggle into string — lasso, whip, crop — joining what has come apart.
3. [[Searles — responding to other persons' unconscious processes]] — Where the self's boundary is incomplete, other people's unconscious gets in.
4. [[Schizophrenia]] — The diagnosis as the library holds it.
5. [[Jung — if I have to suffer, let it be from my reality]] — The circulated passage — not in the book it's attributed to, which is its own kind of split.
6. [[Autobiography and the self]] — The theme: writing the self as a problem.
7. [[My Life and My Life in the Nineties]] — Hejinian builds a self out of sentences that don't agree with each other.
8. [[Benito Cereno]] — Delano misreads everyone, including himself; the novella is a case study in a split the reader shares.
