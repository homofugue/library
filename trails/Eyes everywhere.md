The Thrones in Didron's engraving are wheels covered in eyes. This walk follows the eye from the angelic orders to the surveillance state to the machine that answers back.

1. [[Thrones — fiery two-winged wheels (Didron fig. 152)]] — Two interlocked fiery wheels, every wing an eye — Didron's most complete illustration of the third order.
2. [[Angels]] — The angelic orders as a scheme for seeing the invisible.
3. [[Visions]] — Ezekiel's vision is the source; the genre of seeing what isn't there.
4. [[Simone Martini — Virgin of the Annunciation (Orsini Polyptych)]] — The Virgin looks away from what she has just seen. A vision received, not sought.
5. [[A Scanner Darkly]] — Dick's agent is assigned to watch himself and loses track of which one he is.
6. [[Technology and surveillance]] — The theme the novel opens.
7. [[francisbaken — a terabyte of linear algebra]] — The eye becomes a function: 'this silicon monolith speaks with the tongues of men'.
8. [[Artificial intelligence]] — End on the theme — the newest set of eyes.
