Tyehimba Jess's performers resisted minstrelisation; Rosalía's Lux refuses one language. This walk is about performance as a way of not being pinned down.

1. [[Olio]] — Scott Joplin, the Fisk Jubilee Singers, the McKoy sisters — performers who would not be the show they were booked as.
2. [[Music and performance]] — The theme.
3. [[OSSIA]] — Seo's bilingual debut, addressed to ghosts and built on music's 'alternative passage'.
4. [[Lux]] — Rosalía: orchestral, polyglot, a record about saints that sounds like none of its sources.
5. [[Lux — what language would I even use]] — Your own question about the record — which language a review could even be in.
6. [[On Rosalía]] — Carson, twenty thousand fans, a hangover: the critic as one more body in the hall.
7. [[Translation]] — The theme that runs under all of it.
8. [[Heart Lamp]] — Kannada stories carried into English and given the Booker — performance of a different kind.
