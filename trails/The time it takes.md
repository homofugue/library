Akerman says the compliment people pay a film is that they didn't notice the time pass, and that with her you see it pass and feel it pass. This walk collects the things in the library that do the same.

1. [[Chantal Akerman — I took two hours of someone's life]] — The thesis statement, from a television interview.
2. [[Time and duration]] — The theme.
3. [[Issa — A bath when you're born]] — A whole life in three lines, bracketed by two baths.
4. [[The Book of a Thousand Eyes]] — Hejinian's night works — twenty years of insomnia and lullaby.
5. [[Dreams and sleep]] — Where the night works gather.
6. [[Benito Cereno — shadows, cowls, reserve]] — Melville's becalmed afternoon: somnambulist souls, 'his eye in a dream'. A story in which nothing moves and everything is at stake.
7. [[Du Fu — Early Autumn, Miserable Heat, Papers Piling Up (excerpt)]] — The other kind of duration: the heat, the desk, the papers that keep coming.
