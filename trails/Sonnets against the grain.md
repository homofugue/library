Fourteen lines is a small room. This walk is about poets who moved into it to fight.

1. [[The sonnet]] — The form.
2. [[Olio]] — Jess's syncopated sonnets — the form made to carry ragtime and a double voice.
3. [[Complete Poems (Claude McKay)]] — 'If We Must Die' and the sonnets that helped start the Harlem Renaissance.
4. [[The Tradition]] — Brown's duplex, a sonnet crossed with a ghazal and the blues.
5. [[Not a Force of Nature]] — De'Ath: love sonnets into 'Eight Work Emails' into 'Institutional Critique'.
6. [[Marxism]] — The reading that runs under De'Ath and Reveiz.
7. [[MOPES]] — Reveiz's three acts: revolutionary disappointment arcing toward queer utopia.
8. [[Prosody]] — The theme of metre itself.
9. [[L. S. C. — meritorious in sentiment but defective in metre]] — And the editor who turned someone down for it.
