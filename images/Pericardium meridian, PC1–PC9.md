The pericardium channel from PC1 (Tianchi, beside the nipple) down the inner arm to PC9 (Zhongchong, tip of the middle finger); 9 tsun from axilla to elbow crease, 12 tsun from elbow to wrist. PC6 (Neiguan) is the one you'll meet most — nausea, anxiety, the wrist-band point.
