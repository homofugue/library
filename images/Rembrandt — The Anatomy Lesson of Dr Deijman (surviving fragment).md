What survives of the guild portrait after the 1723 fire in the surgeons' guild room: the praelector's hands opening the brain, the assistant Gijsbert Calkoen (1621–1664) holding the skullcap, and the body of Joris Fonteijn, “Zwarte Jan” (1633/34–1656), a tailor hanged on 29 January 1656 for a knife-point robbery. The foreshortened corpse quotes Mantegna's dead Christ.
